# SUPERSEDED — do not cite

These synthetic-data results were produced by IIP v2.1 (2026-10-17). The v2.1
simulator credited orders one day early (an order placed at the end of day t was
usable before the demand of day t+L, so only L-1 days of lead-time demand were
covered). Simulated service levels were therefore optimistic and shortage costs
low. The optimisation-only tables (gaps to the exact optimum) are unaffected, but
the whole folder is kept only for traceability. Use results/synthetic/<date>/ from v2.2.
